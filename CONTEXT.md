# TransitRail Context

- **Service day**: requested calendar date.
- **Verified timetable**: date-specific departures from a registered official source.
- **No verified timetable**: pair/date cannot be verified; not “no service.”
- **Journey**: direct or transfer movement.
- **Catalog**: discoverable stations/lines with provenance.
- **Publication**: validated public route data; departure claims require verification.

Avoid “fallback schedule” and “indicative” for missing data.
